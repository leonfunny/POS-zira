Kitchen self-order kiosk fonts are copied from npm packages during implementation:

- @fontsource/fraunces@5.2.9
- @fontsource/plus-jakarta-sans@5.2.8

Copied files:
- Fraunces normal weights 500, 600, 700 for latin, latin-ext, vietnamese subsets.
- Plus Jakarta Sans normal weights 500, 600, 700, 800 for latin, latin-ext, vietnamese subsets.

Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see LICENSE-OFL.txt.
These files are committed as local woff2 assets so the kiosk renders offline without runtime CDN or npm font imports.
